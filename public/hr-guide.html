Before doors open

  1. Open the app from your home screen. Tap your own name and enter your 6-digit PIN. Five wrong PINs lock you out for 10 minutes; an admin can unlock you.

  2. On the home screen, check the event name and today's day (for example "Day 1"). If it says No event day today, call the admin before scanning.

  3. Charge your phone, turn the brightness up and keep mobile data or Wi-Fi on. Tap Scan once and allow the camera.

Find the red message in the table below and follow it.

The colour screen clears after 1.5 seconds and the camera is ready again. The same code is ignored for 3 seconds, so you can't double-scan by accident. Tap the screen to clear it sooner.

Reading a code aloud

62B5VW

Six characters: capital letters and digits. Codes never contain 0 O 1 I L. If you hear "O" or "zero", it's probably D or Q. Search accepts lowercase too.

Rules

  • Everything you do is saved under your name.
  • Never share your PIN or hand over your phone while you're logged in.
  • One code lets one person in, once per day.
  • Don't post or photograph attendees' codes; they work like a ticket.
  • When your shift ends: Home → Log out.
